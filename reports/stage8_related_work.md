## Novelty and fair-comparison implications

Do not claim “first gradient-aware world model” or “first execution-aware interface.” Potential claim must isolate the combination of a deployable partially observed command bottleneck, simulator-query-constrained gradient supervision and measurable physical update gains. Reliability weighting itself is not yet a sufficiently differentiated algorithmic contribution.

Compare objective losses on the same EAGM architecture before importing whole agents with different action semantics. For external agents, explicitly match observations, controller memory, reset access, simulator interaction and policy horizon; additionally report computation. Official baseline task adaptation is still required. Hardware limitations have not been benchmarked, so no claim of impossibility is warranted.

Uncertainty-aware optimization remains a literature gap in this first pass: model ensembles, robust policy optimization and calibration guarantees require a focused second review before C3 can be promoted. Equation-level PAML comparison and full SuperMimic controller input inspection are also outstanding. This table does not certify novelty.
